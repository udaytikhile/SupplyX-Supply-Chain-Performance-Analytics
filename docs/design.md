# SupplyX Design System Specification
- **Mood**: Precision executive logistics cockpit—approachable, high-contrast, uncluttered, and immediately intuitive to non-technical leaders.
- **Palette**: Deep Slate (`#0F172A`), Crisp Canvas (`#F8FAFC`), Success Emerald (`#10B981`), Warning Amber (`#F59E0B`), Alert Crimson (`#EF4444`), Brand Cobalt (`#2563EB`).
- **Fonts**: Space Grotesk (Display/Headings, 700/600, `display=swap`) paired with Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & Data Tables, 400/500, `display=swap`).
- **Layout**: Asymmetric KPI summary rail on left/top with card-based drill-down canvas, responsive fluid grid, and high visual hierarchy.
- **Signature Element**: "Fulfillment Velocity & Promised SLA Gap Bar"—an instant visual indicator per route showing scheduled vs actual shipping days with traffic-light status chips.
